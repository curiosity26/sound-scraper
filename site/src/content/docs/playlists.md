The menu at the top left of the library switches between **Library** (every recording) and your playlists.

## Making a playlist

1. Choose **New Playlist…** from the menu and give it a name.
2. Add recordings with **+ Add Recordings…**, with **Add to ▾** on ticked rows, or with **Add to Playlist…** on a recording's right-click menu.

A recording can be in as many playlists as you like. New recordings made while a playlist is showing are added to it.

## Putting it in order

Drag rows by their handle (≡) to reorder the playlist. Clicking a column header sorts the view only; the **#** column is the playlist's own order.

**Remove from Playlist** takes a recording out without deleting it.

## The ⋯ menu

The **⋯** button renames, duplicates or deletes the playlist, or exports it as an `.m3u8` file other players can open.

## Will it fit on a CD?

Under a playlist, a bar shows how much of a 74 or 80 minute CD it fills, counting the gaps between tracks, with a line such as "Fits a 74-minute CD with 21:22 to spare". When it's ready, [burn it](../burning-a-cd/).

## Files that move

Playlists follow renames made in the app. If a file is renamed or removed outside the app, its row shows as missing; put the file back under the same name and it's found again.
