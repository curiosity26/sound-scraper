title: Playback
description: Listen to your recordings in the player.
group: Using the app
order: 6
---

Select a recording in the library and press **PLAY**, or double-click its row.

- Drag the seek bar to jump around.
- Press **STOP** to stop and rewind to the start.
- In a [playlist](../playlists/), playback carries on with the next track.
- The visualizer and meters follow what's playing. Click the visualizer to cycle its styles.

Pressing **REC** at any time stops playback and starts a new recording. While you're recording, REC is disabled and Play turns into Pause.
