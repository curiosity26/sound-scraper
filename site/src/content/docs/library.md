Click **LIBRARY** on the main panel to open the library. It lists every MP3 in your recordings folder with its duration, date, size, artist and album.

## Finding things

- Click a column header to sort; click it again to reverse.
- Type in **Search** to filter by name, title, artist or album.
- Files you add to or remove from the folder yourself show up in the library a moment later.

## Working with a recording

Click a recording to select it and show its tags in the **Details** panel. Double-click a row to play it.

Right-click a recording (or use the details panel's gear menu) for:

- **Edit Track…** opens the [track editor](../track-editor/).
- **Add to Playlist…** puts it in a [playlist](../playlists/).
- **Show in Finder** (Explorer on Windows) shows the file.
- **Change cover…** sets the cover art.
- **Move to Trash** (Recycle Bin on Windows) deletes it.

## Renaming

Click a recording's **File name** in the Details panel and type the new name. Renaming keeps the `.mp3` extension and updates the title tag too, unless you had already given the recording a different title. Playlists follow the rename.
